Steady AR Viewer

Places a 3D model on the floor once, pins it there, and keeps it locked until you choose to move it.

Model

Demo cube (30 cm)

How to use

  1. Start AR and point the phone at the floor, tilted down.
  2. Move slowly side to side until the ring turns green.
  3. Tap Place here. The model is now locked.
  4. Use the sliders to rotate and resize. Tap Move to re-place.
